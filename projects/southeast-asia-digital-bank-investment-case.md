# Southeast Asia Digital Bank - Investment Case and Staged Market Entry

> **Document type:** Externally grounded interview case reconstruction, not a claim of an independently verified completed engagement.
>
> **Timeline alignment:** The [public Corporate Development resume](https://github.com/beastofbayarea/shivam-singh-corpdev-ma/blob/main/shivam-singh-corpdev-ma.pdf) is used only to place this case within the McKinsey role dated July 2014-June 2016.

## Evidence-grounded premise

The 2014 Global Findex documented substantial remaining financial exclusion in East Asia and the Pacific. The 2016 BIS/World Bank PAFI report treated transaction accounts, infrastructure, access, and adoption as connected foundations. MAS's June 2016 sandbox consultation illustrated bounded experimentation with safeguards and exit criteria. These sources support staged investment rather than an all-at-once launch.

## Case approach

- Size reachable underserved segments using account ownership, cash use, payment behavior, and channel access.
- Define an MVP around transaction utility, identification, payments, support, and responsible credit.
- Model base and downside cases for adoption, acquisition cost, loss, funding, and regulatory dependencies.
- Release capital against bounded launch evidence, safeguards, and explicit stop/scale gates.

## Evidence-based success measures

Use active account use, payment success, complaint resolution, acquisition economics, credit performance, safeguard breaches, and regulatory findings. These are proposed measures, not historical results.
