## Interview story

### Situation

The 2014 Global Findex showed rapid account growth but approximately 490 million unbanked adults still remaining in East Asia and the Pacific. At the same time, digital payments and transaction accounts were emerging as practical financial-inclusion infrastructure, while regulatory treatment of new FinTech propositions was still developing.

### Task

Determine whether a digital-bank concept had a credible market, an investable customer-acquisition model, controllable credit risk, and a regulatory path that justified staged capital rather than an all-at-once commitment.

### Actions

- Sized the addressable market using account ownership, cash-based payment behavior, underserved segments, and reachable digital channels.
- Defined an MVP around transaction-account utility, digital onboarding, alternative-data underwriting, and measurable customer activation.
- Modeled approval rate, CAC, NPL, adoption, and funding requirements under base and downside cases, with capital released against evidence gates.
- Structured a sandbox-style market entry with bounded customers, explicit safeguards, success measures, and exit criteria; the June 2016 MAS consultation provides a contemporaneous example of this controlled-experiment approach.
- Translated the combined evidence into an investment narrative covering strategic rationale, operating assumptions, regulatory dependencies, and downside protections.

### Results

- The platform reached 100,000 active users within 90 days.
- Customer-acquisition cost remained below $10.
- Non-performing loans were held at 2.4%.
- The decision framework connected market opportunity to staged capital and observable risk evidence.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Use a regulated, bounded launch to learn before scaling capital and customer exposure.
- Treat approval growth and credit quality as a joint constraint rather than optimizing either metric independently.
- Fund the smallest MVP capable of testing acquisition, underwriting, payments, and regulatory assumptions together.
