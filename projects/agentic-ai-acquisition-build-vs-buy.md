# Agentic AI Acquisition — Build-vs-Buy & Integration Strategy

> **Portfolio lens:** Acquisition diligence, build-versus-buy judgment, synergy modeling, integration planning, and post-deal value creation.

## Executive snapshot

Reframed an $85M agentic-AI acquisition from a margin failure into a conditional value-creation thesis. Independent technical diligence, a cost and pricing bridge, explicit walk-away criteria, and a four-month integration plan connected strategic fit to realizable post-close economics.

## Resume-ready impact

- Led build-versus-buy and cross-functional diligence for an $85M agentic-AI opportunity, establishing a 5% hallucination walk-away threshold and independently validating the target at 3.2%.
- Built an integration value-creation plan that modeled a 70% reduction in inference cost and a gross-margin increase from 55% to 81%.
- Changed the recommendation from stop to acquire with conditions, accelerating time to market from 18 months to four and increasing autonomous resolution from 14% to 72%.

## Interview story

### Situation

The target had strong growth, proprietary workflow data, and years of expert corrections, but its flat-rate pricing and third-party infrastructure made power users uneconomic. Engineering and Finance initially wanted to terminate the deal.

### Task

Determine whether weak current economics reflected a broken asset or a fixable operating model, then define the conditions required for acquisition approval.

### Actions

- Compared internal build and acquisition across time, data moat, expert feedback, execution risk, economics, and defensibility.
- Commissioned an independent red-team of the retrieval and orchestration stack against a pre-agreed walk-away threshold.
- Modeled infrastructure migration, quantization, concurrency, support automation, and hybrid usage pricing.
- Sequenced platform, model, safety, and commercial integration while preserving product velocity.

### Results

- The target passed technical diligence at 3.2% hallucination versus a 5% threshold.
- Inference cost fell 70%, and gross margin reached 81%.
- General availability accelerated from 18 months to four.
- Autonomous resolution reached 72%, and net dollar retention reached 158%.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Separate asset quality from the weakness of its current cost structure.
- Approve the acquisition only with an executable integration and margin bridge.
- Preserve the product architecture while hot-swapping infrastructure and controls.

## Leadership signal

Aligned Finance, AI Engineering, Hardware, Research, and executive leadership around visible assumptions, explicit deal gates, and post-close owners.
