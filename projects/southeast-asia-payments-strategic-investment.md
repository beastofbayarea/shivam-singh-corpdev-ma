## Evidence-grounded premise

The World Bank's Fast Payments Toolkit covers implementation choices including QR codes, APIs, authentication, consumer protection, fraud, interoperability, and pricing. PCI DSS 4.0 provides technical and operational requirements for protecting payment-account data. These sources support valuing a payments target through network utility, merchant adoption, risk controls, and interoperability rather than transaction growth alone.

## Case approach

- Map merchants, consumers, acquirers, issuers, rails, and regulators in the network.
- Separate volume growth from durable economics, interoperability, security, and switching costs.
- Diligence fraud, authentication, dispute handling, data protection, and operational resilience.
- Stage investment and integration around verified merchant utility and control readiness.

## Evidence-based success measures

Use active merchants, repeat payment use, success rate, fraud loss, dispute resolution, interoperability, unit economics, and compliance gaps. These are proposed measures, not historical results.
